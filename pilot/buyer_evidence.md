# Buyer discovery protocol

Status: no interviews, offers, commitments or payments recorded.

Prioritize reachable budget owners over generic market size. For each authorized conversation, record a sanitized evidence ID, date, buyer role, workflow frequency, recent concrete failure, current workaround, estimated burden, source of the estimate, alternatives, approval process, permitted sample availability, offer scope, objection, and next step.

Ask about the last actual occurrence before asking whether an AI product sounds useful. Distinguish reported burden from measured time. Do not interpret willingness to view a demo as willingness to pay.

Evidence ladder: interview interest → sample permission → scoped offer discussion → purchasing commitment → paid delivery → continuation/renewal. Record the actual stage.

First offer to test: one release's documentation drift audit with source-linked findings and reviewer-approved correction proposals. Price and turnaround remain unset pending effort measurements and buyer discussion.

Outreach requires explicit recipient/message authorization. Customer identities, private artifacts and interview transcripts belong in authorized private storage, not this repository.
